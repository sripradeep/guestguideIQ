## Contribution

Reviewed `personas.md` and `stories.md` for persona fidelity and UX quality — not just functional coverage. Overall the epics are functionally sound and several ACs already show good defensive-UX instinct (AC1.5.2's "not yet ready" state, AC2.2.2's graceful sparse-content view, AC2.3.2's sparse-locality chat messaging, AC3.2.1's form-preserves-input-on-error). The gaps below are places where the persona's real-world context should shape the story/AC but currently doesn't.

### Persona fidelity

1. **Missing device/context attribute for Sam (Guest).** `personas.md` says the experience "must work with zero setup" but never states the dominant access context: a traveler opening a texted/emailed link on a phone, often on hotel/property WiFi or roaming data, sometimes standing at the front door. This single fact should drive the whole Guest journey's interaction design (mobile-first layout, resilience to slow/flaky connections, minimal data payload) and currently isn't captured anywhere in personas.md or reflected in any Guest AC. Recommend adding a `Device/Context` line to Sam's persona: "Primarily mobile, first visit to the link, may have weak/roaming connectivity at the property; likely opens the link once, in the moment, not in advance."
2. **Trust/legitimacy friction not acknowledged.** Sam receives a bare link "from the property owner, likely alongside check-in details." Guests are trained to be wary of unsolicited links (phishing conditioning). Recommend a line in Sam's Pain Points: "Wariness of clicking an unfamiliar link — needs immediate visual confirmation (property name/photo) that the link is legitimate and belongs to their actual stay."
3. **Priya's persona would benefit from an explicit device note too** — "comfortable with web apps and file uploads" implies desktop, but PDF upload during onboarding (US1.4) could plausibly happen from a phone (e.g., a scanned guide). Worth confirming intended primary device for onboarding rather than leaving it implicit.

### Story/AC-level UX gaps

4. **US1.3 (Onboarding Wizard) has no save-and-resume behavior.** Priya's persona is explicitly "occasional, bursty use — heavy during onboarding." A multi-step wizard for someone who isn't a full-time user WILL be abandoned mid-way at least some of the time (interruption, needing to find a document, etc.). Neither AC1.3.1 nor AC1.3.2 addresses returning to an in-progress wizard. Recommend adding an AC: "Given I exit onboarding partway through, when I log back in, then I resume where I left off rather than restarting." Progress indication (step X of Y) should also be called out as an interaction requirement, not left implicit.
5. **US1.4 (Import PDF) has no review/confirm step before content goes live.** PDF text/structure extraction is inherently lossy — dumping extracted content straight into a "seeded" guide without letting Priya preview and edit it before it's guest-visible risks a guide full of garbled text going live unnoticed. Recommend an AC: "Given a PDF import completes, when extraction finishes, then I see the extracted content in an editable draft state before any guest can view it, not published automatically." (This also needs a loading/in-progress state — extraction may not be instant — which is currently unaddressed.)
6. **US1.5/US1.6 — no defined experience for a Property Owner whose locality has zero curated POIs yet.** FR5.3/US4.1 acknowledge localities are onboarded progressively by Ops, but no Property Owner-facing story addresses what Priya sees if she signs up for a brand-new locality with nothing curated yet. This is a real near-term scenario (every locality starts at zero) and as written risks an empty, discouraging "browse POIs" screen with no guidance. Recommend an AC under US1.6: "Given my locality has no curated POIs/events yet, when I visit the curation screen, then I see a clear message that content is being added for my area rather than an empty/broken list."
7. **US2.1 only covers the post-checkout expiry edge; the pre-check-in case is undefined.** AC2.1.2 handles "checkout date has passed" but not a guest opening the link before their stay's check-in date (e.g., an eager guest, or the property owner sending the link too early). Recommend clarifying intended behavior — allow early access, or show a "your stay hasn't started yet, come back on [date]" state — since silence here will default to whatever's easiest to implement rather than a deliberate UX choice.
8. **US2.3 (Itinerary Chat) has no defined behavior for LLM/service failure or latency.** AC2.3.2 covers sparse-content gracefully, but there's no AC for the chat service being slow or unavailable (a real risk given OQ3's provider/latency question is still open) — a Guest on flaky connectivity is exactly the user who will hit this. Recommend an AC: "Given the itinerary assistant is slow to respond or unavailable, when I send a message, then I see a clear loading/waiting state and, on failure, a message that lets me retry rather than a silent hang or generic error."
9. **Accessibility is entirely unaddressed across all stories.** No AC in `stories.md` mentions keyboard navigability, screen-reader support, or non-color-dependent status indicators (e.g., link-expired state, subscription status, form errors) anywhere — not even as a general note. Given Sam's "variable" tech comfort and single-shot usage pattern, and Priya's onboarding flow being a first impression, recommend adding a short cross-cutting note (e.g., a "UX & Accessibility Baseline" subsection) stating that all Guest- and Property-Owner-facing surfaces target WCAG 2.1 AA, since this is easy to lose once decomposition starts at `domain-design`/`units-generation` if it isn't anchored here first.
10. **Minor — US1.8 (cancel subscription) UX note.** AC1.8.2 already does the right thing by requiring the Property Owner be told what happens to guide visibility on cancellation — good defensive-UX instinct already present. Flagging only to note it should carry forward: at minimum, guide content should probably remain intact (not deleted) on cancellation so a guide is instantly restorable if she resubscribes, rather than requiring re-onboarding. Worth an explicit assumption note if not already decided elsewhere.

None of the above require new personas or restructuring — they're additive ACs/notes that keep the existing epic-level granularity (per Q6) and can be decomposed further at `domain-design`/`units-generation` like everything else.

## Positions

- AGREE: Persona set (Property Owner, Guest, Admin/Ops) and priority ranking are correct and sufficiently distinct; no fourth persona needed.
- AGREE: Epic-level granularity and persona/journey grouping (Q4, Q6, Q7) are the right call for this stage.
- OBJECT: US1.3's acceptance criteria are incomplete for Priya's "occasional, bursty" usage pattern without a save-and-resume AC (see point 4) — onboarding abandonment is a realistic first-week failure mode as written.
- OBJECT: No story addresses the zero-content-locality experience for a Property Owner (point 6) — this is a near-certain day-one scenario (every locality starts empty) left as a silent gap between US1.6 and US4.1.
- OBJECT: Accessibility has no presence anywhere in `stories.md` (point 9) — this should be anchored at the story level now rather than assumed to survive into decomposition unprompted.
